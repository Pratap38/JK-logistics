## Services

### Same-Day Courier
**One vehicle, your consignment only, collection straight to delivery.**

Some things genuinely cannot wait until tomorrow — the part that has stopped a production
line, the documents that have to be signed today, the replacement unit for a customer who
is already annoyed. We put a vehicle on your job and nothing else. It collects, it drives,
it delivers. No sorting hub, no overnight trailer, no handing over to a second driver
halfway. You get the registration and the driver's number, and the goods arrive exactly as
they left, because nobody else has touched them.

### Multi-Drop Distribution
**Planned rounds, sequenced the night before.**

If you're delivering to thirty customers a week, the gap between a good round and a bad one
is planning. We sequence your drops before the driver starts, so the route runs in a sensible
line instead of criss-crossing the county burning your money in diesel. Your customers get
told roughly when to expect us. You get one collection, one invoice, and one person to ring
when something changes.

### Pallet Freight
**Full loads and part loads, one pallet to twenty-six.**

Everything from our 7.5-tonner upwards carries a tail lift as standard, which means we can
deliver where there's no loading dock and no forklift — building sites, small industrial
units, shops on a high street. If a pallet has to go somewhere awkward, tell us when you
book and we'll send the vehicle that can actually get it off the back.

### Removals & Bulky Goods
**The things too big, too heavy or too valuable for a parcel network.**

Office moves, machinery, white goods, furniture. We bring a box body with a tail lift and a
crew who'll wrap it, load it, and put it back together at the other end. Office relocations
are normally run at a weekend so nobody loses a working day. [CONFIRM: weekend moves]

### Warehousing & 3PL
**Store it with us and we'll deliver it too.**

Pallet and shelf storage at our Luton site, with our team picking, packing and despatching
your orders. It suits you if you've outgrown the garage, if stock arrives by the container
and leaves in ones and twos, or if you'd simply like shipping to stop being your problem.
Same company storing it and moving it means one set of paperwork and one conversation when
something needs chasing. [CONFIRM: racking type and capacity]

# Part 2 — Their business model, mapped onto ours

## How they sell

They slice the same fleet three different ways, so one vehicle earns its money under
several headings:

1. **By urgency** — same day, next day, overnight, pre-booked, regular planned route.
2. **By vehicle size** — small van through to artic, priced on capacity.
3. **By speciality** — awkward loads, hazardous goods, chilled, white glove, two-man.

Roughly two dozen named services sit in three groups: Courier, Freight, Specialist.
Most of them are the same lorries and the same drivers, described in the language a
particular customer already uses.

## How we sell now — and why it costs us work

Our site sells along **one** axis only: the type of job. Same-day courier, multi-drop,
pallet freight, removals, warehousing. Five headings, and that is the whole shop window.

The problem is that customers don't search by job type. They search by the problem they
have this morning. Somebody who needs a pallet in Leeds by tomorrow lunchtime, or a van
and a driver for a day, or a machine that won't fit on a standard trailer, looks at our
five headings and doesn't see themselves. So they ring somebody else.

Nothing needs buying to fix that. The work is already inside what we do — it just isn't
named.

## The one genuinely big opportunity — AOG

**AOG means "Aircraft on Ground."** A grounded aircraft costs an airline thousands per
hour, so when a part is needed the job goes to whoever can collect and deliver *now*,
and price is close to irrelevant. It is the best-paid work in the courier trade.

Voyager sell it from Gloucestershire. **We are minutes from London Luton Airport.**

That is not marketing — it's geography, and it is the single strongest commercial
advantage this business has. Being on the doorstep of a major airport, with the M1 at
junctions 10 and 11 for onward runs, is exactly the profile an AOG customer wants.

Worth understanding before chasing it: airside access needs vetting and a permit, and
most airline and MRO contracts expect a Goods in Transit limit well above a normal
haulier's. Both are obtainable. Neither is free. **This is a conversation to have with
Mr Singh — it could be worth more than everything else on this page put together.**

## What to refuse — and say so plainly

Naming a service we cannot honour is how a small haulier loses a customer permanently.
Our fleet cannot do these, so they stay off the site:

- **Temperature controlled** — no refrigerated units. Don't imply otherwise.
- **ADR / hazardous goods** — needs ADR-licensed drivers and carrier documentation.
- **Import, export, customs clearance** — needs customs agent capability and an EORI setup.
- **European delivery** — needs the above plus an international operator's licence.
- **Car transportation** — no transporter or beavertail in the fleet.
- **Fashion / hanging garment** — no rails fitted.

Turning work down cleanly, then recommending somebody who can do it, wins more repeat
business than bluffing and failing.

# Part 3 — Copy for the new headings

### Full Truck Load
**Your freight, our whole trailer, nothing else on board.**

When you've enough to fill a lorry, sharing one stops making sense. You take the whole
vehicle — 18-tonne rigid or 44-tonne artic — and it goes from your loading bay to the
delivery address without stopping at a depot to be broken down and rebuilt. Fewer hands on
your pallets means less damage and a delivery time we can actually commit to.

### Next Day & Overnight
**Loaded tonight, delivered before you open tomorrow.**

Not everything is an emergency, but most things are still a deadline. Book it during the
day, we collect the same afternoon, and it's delivered the following morning. For trade
counters and shops that means stock on the shelf before the first customer walks in,
without paying same-day rates for it.

### Vehicle & Driver Hire — Full or Half Day
**Book the van and the driver by the day, not by the drop.**

Some days you don't have one delivery, you have a list that keeps changing. Take a vehicle
and driver for a half day or a full day and use them as you need — collections, drops,
a site move, a trade show. You direct the driver; we cover the vehicle, the fuel, the
insurance and the hours. It usually works out cheaper than booking six separate jobs, and
far cheaper than running a van you only need twice a week.

### Oversized & Awkward Loads
**If it won't go through the back doors, we load it from the side.**

Our curtain-sided vehicle opens along its whole length, so a forklift can load from either
side instead of trying to reach in from the rear. That's the answer for long steel,
machinery, glass, timber, packaged units — anything the shape of which is the actual
problem. Send us dimensions and a weight and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits, and
on which vehicle.

### Parcels & Documents
**One item, direct, no sorting hub.**

A single box or an envelope that has to arrive today and cannot risk a parcel network.
It travels in a small van, on its own, with one driver from the collection to the door.
More expensive than a courier label, and the right answer when the contents matter more
than the postage.

### Two-Person Delivery & Installation
**Two of us on the van, because one person cannot carry it.**

Heavy or awkward items going into a home or an office — appliances, furniture, equipment —
delivered by two people who'll carry it to the room it belongs in, unpack it, position it
and take the packaging away. [CONFIRM: whether we crew two-man jobs as standard]

### Planned Routes
**The same run, the same days, the same driver.**

If your deliveries repeat, they shouldn't be re-quoted every week. We set a fixed route on
fixed days at an agreed rate, with a driver who learns your customers, their gate codes and
where the loading bay actually is. You stop booking jobs and start relying on a timetable.
