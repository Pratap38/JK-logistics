Keep Britain’s Goods Moving with JK Logistics

Live visibility and smart routing turn every mile into something you can act on. Our planners, drivers and technology work off the same picture of your freight — so loads leave on schedule, arrive where they should, and tell you exactly where they are in between. No chasing. No guesswork. Just goods that land on time.

Our own fleet

Nine vehicle types, from a small van carrying one pallet to a 44-tonne artic carrying twenty-six. We quote for the vehicle the job actually needs, so you are not paying to move fresh air.

Tail lift as standard

Every vehicle from 7.5 tonnes upward carries one. That means we can deliver where there is no loading dock and no forklift — building sites, small units, shops on a high street.

Minutes from the M1

Our yard sits by junctions 10 and 11 at Luton, a short run to the A1(M) and on the edge of London rather than inside it. Earlier collections, later cut-offs.

The Right Vehicle for the Job

Nine vehicle types, from a small van that will take a single pallet to a 44-tonne artic carrying twenty-six. Tell us the weight, the dimensions and where it is going, and we will tell you which one you need — including when a smaller one will do and cost you less.
