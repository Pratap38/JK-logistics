About us

We move what
Britain buys.

JK Smart Logistic Ltd is a haulage and courier company working out of Luton. We run our own vehicles and our own drivers, and we would rather turn a job down than take it on and let you find out later that we could not do it.

A hub, a fleet,
and people who answer the phone.

Everything we carry passes through one yard in Luton. Loads come in through the afternoon, get sorted overnight and leave before the morning shift starts — which is how a pallet collected in Plymouth at four o’clock reaches Aberdeen the day after next.

We run the vehicles ourselves rather than farming work out to whoever is cheapest that week. It costs more. It also means the driver at your gate knows the site, the paperwork is right, and there is somebody to call who can actually change the plan.

Most of our customers came from somebody else after one delivery too many went missing without a word. So we built the business around the unglamorous part: telling people the truth about where their goods are, early enough to be useful.

Three things we do not bend on

01

Our own fleet, our own drivers

Nothing is subcontracted to whoever is cheapest that morning. The person collecting your goods is on our payroll, in our livery, and answerable to the same standards every day.

02

One person who knows your account

You get a named planner rather than a queue. They know your cut-offs, your sites and the loading bay that needs a tail lift — so you are not explaining it again each time.

03

Tell you before you have to ask

If a load is running late you hear it from us first, with a revised time. Bad news early is worth more than good news that turns out to be wrong.
